# Changelog

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).
Each entry says what players notice; a release's notes show its section.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- A panel beside a Safe's chest screen lists the players online, each with a
  switch, so the owner lets players in or out without the Mods screen. Other
  chests show no panel.
- Shift+L near a Safe you manage names who may open it, in a notice. The key
  replaces the list on the Mods screen, and players can change it there.

### Fixed

- The Ukrainian texts show an apostrophe instead of a box.
- A switch in the panel acts on the Safe whose screen is open, also when you
  opened it from farther than 4 m, not on a nearer Safe you manage.
- A refused switch in the panel names the open Safe's place, not a 4 m reach.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added

- First alpha. Adds the Safe, a chest that only its owner and the players they
  allow can open. Only the owner picks it up.
